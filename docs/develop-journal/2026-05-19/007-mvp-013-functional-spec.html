Why It Changed

MVP-010 and MVP-012 intentionally expose successful commit logs through atomic finalizers rather than the ordinary raw_event_log.append() path. MVP-013 is the smallest visibility proof: committed success logs from those finalizers must be queryable through the same Raw Event Log debug surface as ordinary appended logs.

Review Notes

  • MVP-013 is intentionally a visibility/query proof, not concrete persistence or replay execution.
  • The active spec requires query results to reuse the existing RawEventLogEntry shape and avoid finalizer-only persisted fields.
  • The spec keeps Commit and Effects responsible for committed success log construction and finalizers responsible only for atomic exposure of already-built artifacts.
  • The implementation keeps finalizer visibility opt-in through the test/debug Raw Event Log helper; Commit and Effects modules still do not rebuild query entries or append success logs through the ordinary append path.
  • Future ideas for concrete storage, debug UI, replay execution, pagination, redaction, and retention remain parked in future-slices.html.
