Why It Was Needed

MVP-007 proved one complete USER_MESSAGE turn. MVP-008 starts the CharacterRuntimeState phase with only the read-side foothold from the active spec: a caller-bound current-state source can provide compact current reality to the model through DecisionContext, while durable state storage, mutation, body state, incidents, retrieval, and tools stay out of scope.
